\chapter*{Note on AI Assistance}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Note on AI Assistance}

This thesis was written with the assistance of large language models, specifically Google Gemini 2.5 Pro, which was used exclusively for rephrasing and improving the clarity of written content. All experimental work, including the design and implementation of the system architectures, the execution of experiments, data analysis, and the overall structure and argumentation of this thesis, was conducted entirely by the author. The use of AI was limited to linguistic refinement and did not contribute to the conceptual, methodological, or analytical aspects of this research.
