\chapter{Performance Visualizations and Confusion Matrices}

This appendix provides key performance visualizations and complete confusion matrices from our experimental evaluation. To maintain readability while ensuring reproducibility, we present: (1) comprehensive results for the no-validation baseline configuration across all languages, (2) representative results for the validation configurations (overview plots and English examples), and (3) complete confusion matrices for all configurations. Full visualization sets for all languages and configurations are available in the project repository.
