In summary, the field has progressed from classical HMLC methods to sophisticated hierarchy-aware deep learning models. While recent work in the SemEval-2025 shared task has highlighted the promise of LLM-based prompting, these approaches often rely on monolithic, multi-step prompts. This paper explores a different paradigm: decomposing the HMLC task into a system of independent, specialized agents. We investigate both a parallel agentic framework and a novel actor-critic pipeline designed for enhanced traceability, directly addressing the need for more modular and reliable systems in zero-shot narrative classification.
